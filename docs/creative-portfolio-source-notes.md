## Authorship and presentation

The writing PDF identifies Zehra Ghani as a copy and content writer. The website presents this collection as writing and content work; it does not infer that the writer created every visual asset or brand identity shown in that PDF. The social design PDF is presented as the supplied design collection without inventing an individual designer attribution.

The case pages include the actual supplied artwork in an "Original portfolio selections" gallery, alongside separate generated presentation mockups. These originals retain the supplied brand content, typography and composition. The exports below use only proportional resizing and WebP format conversion, with quality 90 and a maximum edge of 1600 pixels. The artwork has not been rewritten, recoloured or reconstructed. The original writing portfolio cover and biography page are excluded from the public gallery.

No campaign performance, business outcomes or numerical results are inferred from these PDFs. Brand names describe the portfolio examples; no independent client relationship or authorship claim has been added.
